Now

2025 —

I'm the data architect for the Subsecretaría de Espacio Público of Medellín, turning fifteen years of spreadsheets into a governed warehouse — without stopping the operation for a single day. Thirty-four historical data standards, reconciled into one vocabulary, anonymised, and served to both public dashboards and an AI agent that physically cannot reach personal data.

Before that: river logbooks, plant-breeding niche models, edge data-acquisition boards, and a robotic arm. The subject changes. The job doesn't.

Espacio Público data ecosystem

REC 003

Tiered address geocoder

A nine-stage cascade that resolves free-text Colombian addresses — exact nomenclature match, street interpolation, cross-street, axis centroid, reference gazetteer, barrio fallback — and records which stage produced each coordinate, so every point on the map carries its own provenance.

Cobertura86% of 440K records
Resueltas154K free-text addresses
InstrumentosDuckDB spatial · dbt · ArcGIS
