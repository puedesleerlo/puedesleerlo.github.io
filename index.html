III Interests

What I want to work on next

These are the questions I’m pursuing at Carnegie Mellon, building on my experience in credit risk and public-sector AI.

Causal machine learning

Which actions improve an outcome, for whom, and under what conditions? I’m developing the causal-inference and evaluation methods to investigate that.

Decisions under uncertainty

How should a decision change when evidence is incomplete or the environment shifts? I’m interested in connecting probabilistic models to the choices people actually face.

Technical governance of AI

What can a system access and do, when should it stop, and who can intervene? I want to extend my work on data and tool boundaries into controls that can be tested.

My background in physics and philosophy shapes how I think about evidence, uncertainty, and the limits of a model.
